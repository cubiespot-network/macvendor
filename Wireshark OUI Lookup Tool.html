What is an OUI?

The first 24 bits of a MAC address — its first 3 bytes — are assigned by the IEEE to one manufacturer. That block is the Organizationally Unique Identifier.

Longer prefixes

Some vendors hold smaller blocks. This database also includes 28-bit (MA-M) and 36-bit (MA-S) assignments, matched automatically.
